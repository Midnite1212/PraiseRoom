# Design System: Praise Room

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Pixels Behind Glass"**

Praise Room is built from two materials, and both carry meaning. The pixel is the smallest unit of light: square, exact, and the people of a church shining as they worship. Liquid glass reflects like a mirror and overflows its edges, the way worship spills out of the room into a life. Every screen is pixel light seen through layers of glass, over a ground of pure black, or pure white in the light theme.

The mood is calm, precise and luminous. Greys carry no hue at all, and one colour lights the whole app: Your Hue, which each person picks in Settings (Electric Blue by default). Controls are quiet until touched. At rest they are plain glass and grey; under a finger or pointer they fill with liquid light, and their pixel icons glow in Your Hue. The song is the brightest thing on any screen, and nothing in the interface competes with it mid-song.

Density follows the moment. Reading is a single generous column on a phone and two songs side by side on a wide screen. Planning (songs, setlists, folders, settings) is calm, list-led and scannable, with one primary action per page. The layout follows Apple's Liquid Glass guidance: glass is the navigation layer above the content, related controls share one piece of glass, unrelated ones float apart, nested shapes are concentric, and the page still works with reduced transparency and increased contrast.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Black and white, lit by one user-chosen hue; green only for done, red only for what removes or ends.
- Square pixels (icons, progress strips, section markers) against round, rimmed liquid glass.
- Layered glass in three densities (clear, over, strong) that stack by importance.
- Quiet until touched: liquid fills and lit pixel icons appear on hover, press and selection only.
- IBM Plex Sans for words, IBM Plex Mono for music (chords, keys, codes, counts).
- Phone first at 390px, an iPad layout from 768px, the full sidebar layout from 1100px.

## Colors

A hue-free black-and-white world in which a single chosen hue marks what you can act on, what is next, and which pixels are lit.

### Primary

- **Your Hue** (`your-hue`, Electric Blue by default): the one colour of the interface. It marks the selected tab, row or segment (mixed at 32% as a liquid fill), text buttons and links, focus rings, the lit pixels of the next set's strip, switches that are on, chords in the reader, and pixel icons glowing on hover. The light theme uses a deeper value (`your-hue-light`) to hold contrast on white.
- **Alternates** (Ultraviolet, Volt, Signal Orange): each person can swap Your Hue in Settings. Only the hue changes; every rule below stays the same.

### Neutral

- **Ground** (`ground`): pure black; pure white (`ground-light`) in the light theme. The page itself.
- **Lifted Ground** (`ground-lift`): the barely lighter surface for covers and quiet panels.
- **Ink, three steps** (`ink`, `ink-2`, `ink-3`): titles and lyrics; secondary text and resting controls; metadata, hints and counts.
- **Label Grey** (`label`): the reader's section labels and small captions.
- **Hairline** (`hairline`): the only line weight, for dividers, the glass rim and quiet outlines.
- **Glass, three densities** (`glass-clear`, `glass-over`, `glass-strong`): see Elevation & Depth.
- **Fills** (`fill-hover`, `fill-press`): the grey wash under a hovered or pressed control.
- **Pixels** (`pixel-off`, `pixel-played`, `pixel-on`): dark, half-lit and lit squares in every strip of light.

### State

- **Done Green** (`done`): saved ticks and confirmation toasts. Nothing else.
- **Ends Red** (`ends`): removal toasts and the buttons that leave, delete or sign out. At rest those buttons are a whisper of red (45% red mixed into secondary ink); they turn full red on hover, and a final confirm button is solid red.

### Colour Chords

When a reader turns on Colour chords, chord 1 takes Your Hue, chord 4 sea green (`chord-4`), chord 5 warm gold (`chord-5`) and minors violet (`chord-minor`). The light theme has deeper versions of each. These colours exist only inside chord charts.

### Named Rules

**The One Hue Rule.** Your Hue means "act", "next", "selected" or "lit", and nothing else. If an element is none of those, it is grey.

**The Few Meanings Rule.** Green means done; red means something removes or ends. Nothing else gets a colour: not "soon", not folders, not the Home card, not decoration.

**The Hue-Free Grey Rule.** In CSS every grey is written with the `none` hue (`oklch(L 0 none)`), so anything mixed with Your Hue stays true to it instead of swinging through magenta. The tokens above write the same greys as `oklch(L 0 0)` for tooling that can't read `none`.

## Typography

**Display and Body Font:** IBM Plex Sans (with the system sans, then PingFang TC, Noto Sans TC and Microsoft JhengHei)
**Mono Font:** IBM Plex Mono (with ui-monospace, SF Mono, Menlo and Consolas, then PingFang TC and Noto Sans TC)

**Character:** IBM Plex Sans is an engineered grotesque with humane details: open apertures, clear figures and a firm rhythm that stays legible on a phone held or mounted mid-song. IBM Plex Mono is its sibling for music, so every chord takes the same width over its syllable. Chinese falls back to the system's own Traditional Chinese face.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (700, `clamp(1.75rem, 1.2rem + 2.2vw, 2.5rem)`, 1.08): the song title in the reader.
- **Page Title** (700, 1.5625rem, 1.08): the title of every app page, in the 64px header row.
- **Pane Title** (700, 1.375rem): a song, setlist or folder title inside a preview or slide-over.
- **Card Title** (700, 1.25rem): the next set on Home, the setlist card title.
- **Present Title** (700, `clamp(2.5rem, 6.5vw, 6rem)`, 1.02) and **Present** (600, 3rem, 1.3): the song title and lyrics on a TV in Present mode.
- **Row Title** (600, 1rem): titles in lists.
- **Lyrics** (400, 1.125rem default, 1.45): five sizes from 0.9375rem to 1.5rem, chosen in Aa and Settings.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.5): prose in sheets and empty states, kept under 66 characters a line.
- **Control** (600, 0.9375rem): buttons, navigation and toasts.
- **Group Heading** (600, 0.9375rem, secondary ink, with a mono count): "Then 2", "Songs", "Playing". One heading style across every page.
- **Label** (600, 0.875rem) and **Caption** (400, 0.8125rem): field labels, hints and metadata.
- **Section Label** (700, 0.75rem, 0.12em tracking, uppercase, after a 6px pixel in the chord colour): VERSE 1, CHORUS. The same in the reader, the editor and every preview.
- **Chord** (Mono 600, 1em of the lyric size) and **Mono** (600, 0.8125rem): chords, keys, dates in tiles, song codes and counts.

### Named Rules

**The Mono Is Music Rule.** IBM Plex Mono sets musical data and codes only: chords, keys, key offsets, time signatures, song codes, dates in tiles and counts. Words are always set in Sans.

**The Titles Name, Content Carries Rule.** Page titles are a modest 25px and identical on every page; the song is what carries the weight.

## Layout

The app is one responsive build with three layouts.

- **Phone (under 768px):** one column up to 760px wide with an 18px page gutter. A floating top bar (brand, back button, profile record) sits 10px from the top edge, and a glass tab bar of four tabs floats 12px above the bottom with the page's one action as a 56px round button above it on the right. Search and filter stick under the top bar.
- **iPad (768–1099px, either orientation):** the sidebar shows, folded to its 72px icon form, and two-column pages stack. A song preview, setlist preview or open folder slides in from the right as a 420px glass panel over the list. The setlist editor's Add songs panel moves below the songs.
- **Wide (1100px and up):** a 232px glass sidebar (brand row, navigation island, the next set, profile) that folds to 72px with ⌘\. The page fills the rest, up to 1040px wide, or 1200px when the sidebar is folded, so content always takes the room it gives back. Two-column pages put the list beside its detail: songs with a 400px preview, setlists with the calendar, folders with the open folder, Settings with its 400px live preview, and the setlist editor with a 380px Add songs panel.

Every page header is a 64px row whose centre lines up with the sidebar's brand row. A back link takes that same row on sub-pages, with the title below it. Settings and Account rows put the label in a 224px column and every control in one aligned column beside it; on phones the control drops under its label at full width.

The reader keeps one song in a column on phones, with a dock at the bottom. On wide screens two songs stand side by side with an optional 280px setlist sidebar.

Spacing steps through 2, 4, 8, 16, 24 and 40px: tight inside a group, 40px between groups.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is layered glass. The content layer (lyrics, lists, forms) is flat and never sits on a card or on glass. Above it, glass comes in three densities that stack by importance. **Clear** glass (34% tint) holds islands, the top bar's groups and the next-set card. **Over** glass (60%) holds the tab bar and the reader's dock, which float over moving content. **Strong** glass (72%) holds sheets, popovers, live trays, slide-overs and toasts, which must read over anything.

Each piece of glass blurs and saturates what is behind it, carries a soft sheen at the top and has a single hairline rim. In Chromium it also refracts at its rounded edge through an SVG displacement map, with frosted glass as the fallback everywhere else. Under reduced transparency, or increased contrast, glass becomes near-opaque and the rims and secondary text strengthen.

Selection and hover are a thinner layer still: liquid fills. A selected tab, row or segment is a drop of glass tinted with Your Hue, with a highlight at the top that follows the pointer, a light top rim and Your Hue pooling at the bottom. A hovered control gets the same drop at half strength, in grey.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Glass lift** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -10px var(--shadow), 0 2px 8px -2px var(--shadow)`): under every piece of glass.
- **Action button** (`box-shadow: 0 14px 28px -10px oklch(0 0 none / 0.7), 0 2px 6px -2px oklch(0 0 none / 0.5)`): under the phone's round add button.
- **Slide-over** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--hairline), 0 24px 60px -20px var(--shadow)`): under the iPad's slide-in panels.

### Named Rules

**The Song Never Sits on Glass Rule.** Reading text lives on the ground. Glass only ever holds controls, and content scrolls under it into a soft edge band.

**The Hairline Rim Rule.** A glass edge is one quiet hairline. It never gets a bright glow or a white highlight running around it.

**The Densest Glass Wins Rule.** The more a layer must be read over moving content, the denser its glass: clear for islands, over for the dock and tab bar, strong for sheets and trays.

## Shapes

Two shapes, never mixed. Pixels are perfectly square (0 radius): icons drawn on a 24-unit pixel grid, progress strips, section-label markers, the strength meter and loading dots. Glass and everything touchable is round. Controls and pills are full capsules (999px); tiles (date, key, fields) are gently squared at 14px; list rows 16px; navigation islands 24px; the next-set card 28px; sheets, trays and slide-overs 32px. Nested shapes are concentric, so an inner radius equals the outer radius minus the gap between them.

**The Square Pixel, Round Glass Rule.** A pixel is never rounded, and glass is never square.

## Components

Quiet until touched: plain glass and grey at rest, liquid light and lit pixels under a finger or pointer, and one exact place for everything.

### Buttons

- **Shape:** full capsules (999px); 44px tall on phones, 40px in dense desktop headers.
- **Primary:** an ink pill (white on black, black on white) with its icon on the left. One per page or sheet: Add song, New setlist, Done, Create.
- **Ghost:** a grey fill pill for secondary actions (Cancel, Add people, Change). The small version is 36px.
- **Text button:** Your Hue text with a trailing chevron for links within a page ("All songs ›", "Account ›").
- **Leave, Delete, Sign out:** quiet red at rest, full red with a red-glowing icon on hover. The final confirm in a dialog is a solid red pill.
- **Press:** a capsule shrinks to 96% with a springy ease (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.32, 0.52, 1)`).

### Icon Buttons and Pixel Icons

- **Style:** a 40px capsule (44px on phones) with a 24-unit pixel icon; 48px on glass in the top bar.
- **Hover:** a liquid grey fill, and the icon itself lights in Your Hue with a soft glow. Text labels never light up, and current or selected buttons don't glow.
- **Remove vs close:** taking something off a list uses a minus in a ring; a plain × only ever closes.

### Navigation

- **Wide:** a clear-glass sidebar island with Home, Songs, Setlists and Folders as 44px rows with mono counts. The current page is a liquid fill in Your Hue. The next set sits in its own card below, and the profile record at the foot.
- **Phone:** a floating tab bar of four tabs over glass. The current tab is a liquid pill in Your Hue.
- **Back:** a "‹ Parent" text link in the page-header row on wide screens, and a round glass back button at the top left on phones.

### Lists and Rows

- **Row:** 64px minimum, 16px radius. A tile leads, then title and metadata, then a trailing action. Hover is a grey liquid fill; selected is a liquid fill in Your Hue.
- **Date tile and key tile:** 52px squares at 14px radius, a mono number over a small mono caption (29 / Oct, G / 3/4). The next set's tile is plain like every other; its lit strip marks it.
- **Strip of light:** one 6px square pixel per song under each setlist. Lit in Your Hue for the next set, half-lit once played, dark while waiting.
- **Key chip:** a small 24px rounded square in mono, used in dense tables and previews.
- **Folder tag:** a grey capsule with a pixel folder icon. It truncates at 24 characters, with the full name on hover.

### Segmented Controls and Switches

- **Segmented:** a grey capsule track with 3px of padding; options are 36px tall, and the chosen one is a liquid pill in Your Hue. In the reader's top bar, segments sit in 48px glass with 42px options. Labels pair a pixel icon with a word where an icon alone isn't clear (Light, Auto, Dark; Slow, Medium, Fast).
- **Switch:** 46 × 28px; the track turns Your Hue when on, and the knob springs across.

### Fields and Search

- **Field:** a 48px box at 14px radius on a grey fill with a hairline inset. Its label sits above, and password fields have a show/hide button.
- **Focus:** a 2px Your Hue ring inside the box.
- **Error:** a 2px red ring with a red message under the field. New passwords show a four-pixel strength meter.
- **Search:** a 48px glass capsule with a pixel magnifier and a clear button, sticky at the top of its list.

### Sheets, Dialogs, Trays and Slide-overs

- **Sheet (phone):** strong glass rising from the bottom with a 32px radius: a title row with its icon, a body that fades at the bottom while there's more below, and a footer of buttons. It lifts above the on-screen keyboard.
- **Dialog and popover (wide):** create and confirm dialogs sit in the middle; menus anchor to their button.
- **Live trays (reader):** Aa and Key open as trays without a scrim, so the song stays readable behind them. They close on a tap outside, a swipe down or Esc.
- **Slide-over (iPad):** a 420px strong-glass panel from the right for a preview or an open folder.

### Reader

- **Song header:** the display title with an info button centred on its first line, then the artist and the key pill.
- **Chords:** IBM Plex Mono in the chord colour, set above their syllable. The pieces of one word never wrap apart. Section labels are pixel-marked small capitals.
- **Dock:** over glass: Aa, play/pause for auto-scroll, a pill naming Up next (or Previous on the last song) between ‹ and ›, and the key pill when chords are on.
- **Top bar (wide):** the set name on the left; one song or two, Lyrics | Chords, play, Aa and Reading settings grouped on the right, then share.

### Editors

- **Song editor:** a tap-a-word chord editor. Every line has a lead-in slot in a left gutter and an end slot after its last word, so chords with no syllable get a place. An "Edit as: Tap chords | Type as text" row sits above the lyrics.
- **Setlist editor:** songs with a drag handle, a per-set key stepper (− key +, with the offset in Your Hue), and notes.
- **Saved:** one quiet "✓ Saved" at the right end of the page header, in green.

### Empty and Loading States

- **Empty:** a bold one-line statement that names the search, a one-sentence hint, and a way out (Clear search, Clear filters).
- **Loading:** four pixels running in Your Hue beside "Searching…", over skeleton rows that breathe gently; they sit still under reduced motion.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** keep one hue: Your Hue for act, next, selected and lit, and grey for everything else.
- **Do** write every grey with the `none` hue (`oklch(L 0 none)`).
- **Do** use liquid fills for selected and hovered controls, in Your Hue for selected and grey for hover.
- **Do** light pixel icons in Your Hue on hover, never their text labels.
- **Do** give every touchable element 44px on phones, enlarging the hit area rather than the look where needed.
- **Do** line page titles and back links up with the sidebar's brand row in a 64px header.
- **Do** put "Saved" at the right end of the page header, and only there.
- **Do** show Verse 1 first in song previews, falling back to the first section.
- **Do** set chords, keys, dates and codes in IBM Plex Mono.

### Don't:

- **Don't** colour "soon", folders, the Home card or anything decorative.
- **Don't** add a bright glow or white highlight around the edge of glass.
- **Don't** put lyrics or reading text on glass or on a card.
- **Don't** round a pixel or square off glass.
- **Don't** use a plain × to remove something from a list; × only closes.
- **Don't** draw tempo as anything but the four-pixel meter.
- **Don't** let a chord split its word across lines.
