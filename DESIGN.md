# Design System: Praise Room

> **Status.** This world is recorded from its one implementation, the public setlist view design mock (`.impeccable/mocks/praise-room-public-setlist-view.html`, a standalone file that shows the phone in portrait and the desktop in landscape). The production UI (`ui/`) has not adopted it yet: `ui/src/styles.css` still carries the provisional navy palette, which this system replaces. The mock embeds Latin subsets of Geist (400–700) and Geist Mono (500–700) so it works offline; production must self-host the full fonts.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Set as a Strip of Light"**

The song owns the screen. The controls float above it as separate pieces of liquid glass, grouped by what they do, with no full-width bar behind them. The palette is black and white with one accent, electric blue: pure black ground (white in the light theme), hue-free greys, and blue only where something is lit. The set is drawn as a strip of square pixels, one per song: songs still to come are dark, played songs are half-lit white, and the current song is lit in electric blue and glowing. On phones the strip is a row of thin segments under the top controls. On wide screens it is the pixel beside each song in the sidebar, and on a TV it runs along the foot of the screen.

The density is that of a reading instrument. On a phone the lyrics run in one column of up to 760px. On wide screens two songs stand side by side: the current one and the next, ready to slide over. Reading text never sits on glass or a card. Content dissolves into a scroll-edge band before it reaches the floating controls. Dark is the default theme, for dim rooms and stages. The light theme has the same structure, on white, with a deeper blue.

Two materials carry the brand. The first is the pixel, sharp and square: the smallest unit of light. The second is liquid glass: round, rimmed, refracting, and springy in motion. Pixels are never rounded. Glass is never square.

The layout follows Apple's Liquid Glass guidance (developer.apple.com, "Adopting Liquid Glass"). Glass is the navigation layer above the content. Related controls share one piece of glass, and unrelated ones float apart. Nested shapes are concentric. The page works with reduced transparency, increased contrast and reduced motion.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Black, white and hue-free greys, with one accent (`--accent`, electric blue). Colour chords, an opt-in reading aid, is the only other colour.
- Controls float as glass groups; the reading surface is bare ground.
- Every essential control a phone player needs mid-song is in the bottom row, in thumb reach.
- Square pixels mark the set, sections, the original key, pins and the brand mark. A pixel's state is told by brightness alone: off, played, on.
- Pixelarticons (MIT) are drawn at the `--icon` size (24px at 1x, 18px at 2x, 20px at 2.5x and up) with `shape-rendering: crispEdges`.
- Motion uses two curves: a calm ease-out for colour and fades, and a liquid overshoot for anything that moves like glass.
- Geist sets every word, controls included. Geist Mono sets musical data and numbers only. Both fall back to PingFang TC and Noto Sans TC for 繁體中文.

## Colors

The palette is black and white with one accent. Every neutral is hue-free: `oklch(L 0 0)`, or pure white or black at an alpha. The accent is a single token, `--accent`, and every coloured token derives from it: `chord` and `pixel-on` are the accent, `fill-on` is the accent mixed to 32% (14% in light), and `pixel-glow` to 80% (40% in light). Changing `--accent` recolours the whole interface. The frontmatter shows each token resolved. The user chose this palette over the earlier ink-blue world (deep navy ground, everything tinted toward hue 250), which is retired.

### Accent
- **Electric Blue** (`accent`, `chord`): the default and the one saturated colour. It is `oklch(0.68 0.21 262)` on black and a deeper `oklch(0.52 0.26 264)` on white, to keep contrast. It is used for chords, the focus ring, the selected key cell, the offset badge, the switch's on track, the pixel mark, the Edit order and Reset text buttons, the sidebar's Pinned and Next tags, the pin pixel, and toast icons.
- **Lit pixels** (`pixel-on`, `pixel-glow`): the current song, in the accent. On the phone's progress segments it also carries `pixel-glow`, the only glow in the system.
- **Selection** (`fill-on`): the accent as a wash. It marks the segmented thumb (with an accent ring), the songs on screen in the sidebar and the set row, the pressed setlist toggle, and selected options.
- **Alternative accents** (experiments, switchable in the showcase): Ultraviolet `oklch(0.7 0.2 300)` / `oklch(0.5 0.26 300)`, Volt `oklch(0.9 0.21 128)` / `oklch(0.55 0.17 140)`, and Signal orange `oklch(0.73 0.19 45)` / `oklch(0.58 0.21 38)`, dark / light. Each swaps only `--accent` (`data-palette` on `<html>`).

### Neutral
- **Ground** (`ground`, `ground-lift`): pure black, with a single wash from `ground-lift` (0.17) at the top. The light theme is pure white with a 0.955 wash.
- **Ink** (`ink`, `ink-2`, `ink-3`): primary text (0.98), secondary text (0.78: artist, meta, unselected segments, section chips), and tertiary text (0.62: counts, hints, row numbers, the title's info button). Light: 0.13, 0.38 and 0.5.
- **Section labels** (`label`): Verse and Chorus names and their 6px pixel, in a quiet grey (0.74; 0.34 in light), so the accent stays for chords.
- **Played pixels** (`pixel-played`): white at 0.52 (black at 0.45 in light), so a played song is half-lit without taking the accent.
- **Glass Tints** (`glass-tint`, `glass-tint-over`, `glass-tint-strong`): neutral greys under each glass backdrop. The 0.56-alpha tint is for the top groups and the sidebar. The 0.74 tint is for glass over moving lyrics: the bottom groups and the auto-scroll pill. The 0.82 tint is for sheets, popovers and the toast, and is the solid fallback.
- **Rim** (`rim-hi`, `rim-lo`, `rim-edge`): the specular rim gradient and its constant 1px edge.
- **Fills** (`fill-hover`, `fill-press`): white at 0.09 and 0.15 (black at 0.06 and 0.11 in light), for controls inside glass and on the ground (the key pill, gear and section chips).
- **Hairline** (`hairline`): the only divider and border: section chips, the split's centre line, the end of the set, and the footers of the sidebar and song list.
- **Pixel Off** (`pixel-off`): upcoming songs, and the off track of a switch.

### Colour Chords
An opt-in reading aid in Chord settings, off by default. It colours chords by function: the 1 chord in the accent (`chord-1`), the 4 in `chord-4` (green, hue 165), the 5 in `chord-5` (amber, hue 80; 70 in light), minors in `chord-minor` (violet, hue 305), and every other chord in `ink-2`. When an alternative accent sits near one of these, that one moves: minors turn blue (hue 220) under Ultraviolet, the 4 turns cyan (200) under Volt, and the 5 turns yellow (100) under Signal orange. These colours tint chord text only, never a surface, a control or a song.

### Increased Contrast
Under `prefers-contrast: more`, glass tints go near-opaque (0.95–0.97 alpha), `ink-2` and `ink-3` move toward `ink`, `hairline`, `rim-edge` and `pixel-off` roughly double in strength, `fill-on` deepens (44% dark, 24% light), and the light theme darkens the colour-chord tokens to lightness 0.42–0.45.

### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** Black, white and hue-free greys carry the interface; one accent, `--accent`, lights what matters (chords, the current song, selection, focus). Every coloured token derives from `--accent`, never from a hue of its own. No song, section or state gets a colour of its own. Colour chords is the one, opt-in exception, and it touches chord text alone.

**The Light by State Rule.** A set pixel tells where a song sits in the set by brightness alone: dark (`pixel-off`) to come, half-lit (`pixel-played`) played, lit (`pixel-on`, glowing on the phone's segments) now.

**The Chord Carries the Light Rule.** `chord` (the accent) is the only saturated text colour by default. Labels sit a step quieter. Body text, titles and controls stay in ink.

## Typography

**Display and Body Font:** Geist (with the system sans, then PingFang TC, Noto Sans TC, and Microsoft JhengHei)
**Mono Font:** Geist Mono (with ui-monospace, SF Mono, Menlo, and Consolas, then PingFang TC and Noto Sans TC)

**Character:** Geist is a neutral, precise grotesk with open apertures and even colour, legible at a glance on a phone held or mounted mid-song. It sets every word in the world, from the bold song title down to button labels and captions, so the interface reads as one voice. Geist Mono, its sibling, is reserved for music and numbers: chords get the same width each, so they sit predictably over their syllables, and keys and counts don't shift when they change. CJK text falls through to the platform's 繁體中文 face in both stacks. The user chose Geist and Geist Mono after trying Funnel, then Overpass with Source Code Pro.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Geist 700, 1.08, -0.03em, balanced wrap): the song title only. It is `clamp(1.75rem, 1.2rem + 2.2vw, 2.5rem)` on phones, `clamp(2rem, 1rem + 1.8vw, 3rem)` for one song on wide screens, and `clamp(1.75rem, 0.9rem + 1.6vw, 2.625rem)` in each split pane.
- **Lyrics** (400, 1.45): 1.125rem times the text size, which steps through 87.5%, 100% (the default), 112.5%, 130%, 150% and 175%. Lines with chords keep each chord over its word.
- **Chord** (Geist Mono 600, 1em of the lyric size, 1.3): chord symbols, with superscripts at 0.68em.
- **Subtitle** (400, 1rem, `ink-2`): the artist line.
- **Title** (600, 1rem, 1.3): sheet and popover headings and the sidebar's "Setlist".
- **UI Strong** (600, 0.9375rem): the set name in the wide top row, the Aa label, the current song row's title, Done, and the toast.
- **Label** (600, 0.875rem): segment options (Lyrics / Chords and the others in panels), the key pill, the Up next song name, set row items and text buttons.
- **Caption** (400, 0.8125rem): dates, meta, section chips (at 600), panel subheads, row hints, and the "Song 2 of 6 ⌄" pane chooser (at 600). Hints, the Up next caption, keycaps and fact labels are 0.75rem.
- **Mono** (Geist Mono 600, 0.8125rem): song-row numbers and keys, and the 1 4 5 / A B C segment. The same face sets the key pill's key (1rem, 700), the offset badge (0.75rem, 700), the key panel's big key (2.25rem, 700), the key grid (1rem, 600), steppers (0.875rem), the progress count, keycaps, and the TV's "Song 2 of 6".
- **Section Label** (700, 0.75rem, 0.12em, uppercase, 1): song section names only, led by a 6px square pixel. On the TV it is `clamp(0.875rem, 1.2vw, 1.25rem)` with 0.14em tracking.
- **Present** (TV): lines at 600, 1.3, -0.015em, sized to fit the screen from 3rem; the title slide at 700, `clamp(2.5rem, 6.5vw, 6rem)`, 1.02, -0.035em.

### Named Rules
**The One Sans Rule.** Geist sets every word: titles, lyrics, artist, setlist names, song titles, meta, and every control label. Lyrics and body are regular 400; song titles are 700. Weight and size make the hierarchy; don't add a second sans, a display family, or a Light weight.

**The Mono Is Music Rule.** Geist Mono sets musical data and numbers only: chords, key names, offsets, the key grid, the 1 4 5 / A B C segment, row numbers and keys, counts, steppers and keycaps. Never use it for a control's word label, prose, headings, section names, or song titles. Accidentals are spelled `b` and `#` (`Bb`, `F#m`), because neither face has ♭ or ♯ glyphs and a fallback glyph would break the monospace rhythm.

**The Optical Centre Rule.** Geist and Geist Mono centre their capitals in the line box, so labels centre in fixed-height controls with no padding nudge. The title's info button centres on the title's first line: `margin-top: calc(var(--title-size) * 0.54 - 20px)`. Measure again before changing a font, size or control height.

## Layout

Floating glass groups are arranged around a bare reading surface. The wide layout applies from 900px wide in landscape (`(min-width: 900px) and (orientation: landscape)`); everything else, including a tablet held upright, gets the phone layout.

### Phone (portrait)
- **Top row** (52px, `max(10px, safe-area)` from the top and sides): Lyrics | Chords on the left and Copy link on the right, each its own 48px glass capsule. The set is chosen once, so only these two sit at the top.
- **Progress segments:** one 4px pixel segment per song, 4px apart, 6px under the top row and inset 14px, with "2/6" in Mono `ink-3`. Each segment is a 20px-tall button that jumps to its song.
- **Bottom row** (60px, `max(12px, safe-area)` from the bottom, at most 560px wide, 6px gaps): three glass groups in thumb reach. Aa (52 × 56px), then Up next (fills the rest), then the key pill (in Chords only). Each opens its panel upward from itself.
- **Reading column:** at most 760px wide with an 18px gutter. Top padding clears the top row plus 40px; bottom padding clears the bottom row plus 48px.
- **Scroll edges:** a top band (`ground` at 88%, 6px blur, solid for 78% of its height) covers the top row and the segments once the page scrolls, and a bottom band fades the lyrics before the bottom row.
- **Swipe:** a horizontal swipe of more than 70px within 600ms changes songs. It is a hidden extra; the buttons are the way.

### Wide (landscape, 900px and up)
- **Top row:** the setlist toggle (a 48px glass circle), the pixel mark, the set name and the date (plain text on the page), then Lyrics | Chords, the chord settings (1 4 5 / A B C and ⚙, in Chords only), Aa and Copy link on the right, each its own glass group.
- **Frame:** the top row, the sidebar and the stage share one frame: 10px from the window edges (or the safe area), at most 1600px wide and centred (`--frame-l`, `--frame-r`). On screens wider than 1620px the whole layout centres together, so the setlist toggle always sits directly above the sidebar.
- **Setlist: sidebar or song bar.** The setlist is a 280px glass sidebar 10px under the top row, running to 12px from the bottom, with a 24px radius. The ☰ toggle folds it into a set row (and back), remembered per device: a 48px glass row 10px under the top row, spanning the frame, and the songs below take the full width. It never disappears: the set is always in view.
- **Split songs** (the default): a two-column stage from under the top row to the bottom, beside the sidebar. Two songs stand side by side with equal weight, the left one and the one after it, with a hairline between them. Each pane scrolls on its own, with 16px and 48px fades at its top and bottom edges, and each has its own key. → slides the right song over and brings in the next.
- **One song** (Aa → Layout): one scrolling column up to 1240px.
- **Lyric columns** (Aa → Lyric columns, wide screens only): one or two columns of lyrics, in either layout. Each layout remembers its own choice: one column per pane when split, two for one song by default. Columns sit 56px apart for one song and 32px apart inside a split pane, and a section never breaks across columns.
- The phone's progress segments and bottom row are hidden; their jobs move to the sidebar, the top row and the keyboard (← → change songs, Page Up/Down and Space scroll, P presents).

**The Song Owns the Screen Rule.** The lyrics start above the fold at 390px. Chrome floats; nothing pushes the song down except the title, artist, chord controls and section chips.

**The Thumb Rule.** On a phone, everything a player reaches for mid-song (text size, the next song, the key) is in the bottom row. The top holds only what is set once.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from glass over a calm ground. There are no stacked cards. The ground is one even field with a single soft wash of light from the top: a vertical gradient from `ground-lift` at the top edge to transparent by 46vh, over `ground`. There are no radial glows, no textures, and no decorative pixels in the ground. Glass floats over it with a two-layer soft shadow and refracts the lyrics that scroll beneath.

Glass is built as a hybrid.
- **Every browser:** a CSS stack. The host is an isolated stacking context. `::after` carries `backdrop-filter: blur(var(--glass-blur, 14px)) saturate(185%) brightness(1.04)` and the tint. `::before` is the 1px specular rim (a 150° gradient plus the edge colour, masked to the border). Sheets and popovers blur at 24px.
- **Chromium only:** bezel refraction. `::after` swaps to a per-element SVG filter: a Gaussian blur (`data-blur`: 3 for the floating groups and the sidebar, 6 for the toast and the TV controls, 8 for sheets and popovers), then an `feDisplacementMap` from a size-specific displacement map, then saturation, then a specular map blended on top.
  - The displacement follows Snell's law (ior 1.5, thickness 36) over a convex-squircle surface `(1 − (1 − x)^4)^(1/4)`, sampled 96 times.
  - Maps are computed for the bezel band only. The centre is left undisplaced, so text read through the glass stays straight.
  - Maps are cached by width, height, radius, bezel and blur. Same-size controls share one filter.
  - Resizes go through a ResizeObserver, debounced 90ms and then flushed in one animation frame.
- **Fallbacks:** under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`, glass is a solid `glass-tint-strong` with no backdrop filter. Without backdrop-filter support it uses the strong tint. Under increased contrast the tints go near-opaque. Under forced colours glass gets a 1px `CanvasText` border.
- **Rejected:** WebGL glass and SVG-only glass.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Glass float** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -10px var(--shadow), 0 2px 8px -2px var(--shadow)`): every glass surface.
- **Current pixel glow** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 8px var(--pixel-glow)`): the current song's progress segment.
- **Thumb ring** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px color-mix(in oklch, var(--accent) 30%, transparent)`): the segmented thumb.
- **Lifted row** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -12px var(--shadow)`, `glass-tint-strong`, a `rim-lo` outline): a song row while it is dragged.

### Named Rules
**The Glass Floats Rule.** Only the navigation layer is glass: the floating control groups, the sidebar, sheets, popovers, the toast and the auto-scroll pill. Lyrics, titles, the set name and the split panes sit on bare ground.

**The Group, Don't Bar Rule.** There is no full-width bar. Related controls share one piece of glass (Lyrics | Chords; ‹ Up next ›), and unrelated controls float apart, 6–8px from each other. The rows that hold them have no glass and let clicks through.

**The No Glass-in-Glass Rule.** Glass never sits on glass. Controls inside a glass group or panel are plain fills (`fill-hover`, `fill-press`, `fill-on`) or bare taps.

**The Denser Over Motion Rule.** Glass over moving lyrics (the bottom groups and the auto-scroll pill) uses `glass-tint-over`, so its own labels always win.

## Shapes

There are two geometries, and they never trade places.

**Glass and controls are round.**
- Capsules (999px) are used for every floating group, tap, segment, pill, chip, badge, switch, set row item, Done and the toast.
- Sidebars are 24px, sheets and popovers 32px, rows inside sheets 16px, sidebar rows 14px, key cells 12px, text buttons and the pane picker 10px, grips 8px, and keycaps 5px.

**Pixels are hard squares with 0 radius.**
- Progress segments are 4px tall and the TV's are 22 × 4px.
- Song pixels are 10px in rows, and end-of-set pixels are 8px.
- The section pixel is 6px, the pin pixel 6px, the original-key pixel 4px, and the grip's dots 3px.
- The brand mark is a pixel cross drawn at 20px.

Icons are pixelarticons on a 24-unit grid, `fill="currentColor"`, `crispEdges`, sized by `--icon`: 24px at 1x, 18px at 2dppx and up, and 20px at 2.5dppx and up.

**The Concentric Rule.** A shape nested in another keeps the same centre of curvature: inner radius = outer radius − inset.
- A 48px top capsule (24px) holds 42px segment buttons 3px in (21px).
- A 56px bottom capsule (28px) holds 44px controls 6px in (22px).
- A 32px sheet with 16px padding holds 16px rows.
- A 24px sidebar with 10px padding holds 14px rows.

The sidebar's 24px corner matches the 48px capsules in the top row, so the two read as one family.

**The Square Pixel Rule.** A pixel is never rounded, never blurred into a dot, and never drawn at a size where it spans a fractional number of device pixels. Icons take `--icon`, never a fixed size.

## Components

### Floating Glass Groups
The base of the chrome. Each group is a glass capsule with its own refraction (`data-blur` 3). Tap targets inside are bare until hovered (`fill-hover`), and shrink to `scale(0.94)` when pressed (260ms, liquid curve).
- **Top row:** 48px groups. Single controls (setlist toggle, gear, Aa, Copy link) are 48px circles. The setlist toggle carries a `fill-on` wash while the sidebar shows.
- **Bottom row:** 56px groups on `glass-tint-over`. Aa is 52px wide, so Up next keeps whole titles at 390px.
- **Focus:** a 2px solid `chord` outline offset by 2px, everywhere.

### Segmented Control with Liquid Thumb
Options are capsules with Label type: 42px with 16px padding for Lyrics | Chords, 34px with 14px padding in the song header (the Mono 1 4 5 / A B C segment), and 30px with 10px padding in panels. Unselected text is `ink-2`; the selected option is `ink`, with a 200ms colour change. Behind them, a capsule thumb (`fill-on` plus the thumb ring) springs its `translateX` and `width` over 480ms on the liquid curve. It is placed without animation the first time, and re-syncs on resize and after fonts load. Inside panels and the header the track is `fill-hover`; Lyrics | Chords sits straight on its glass.

### Song Header
- **Row 1:** the song title (Display) and a 40px info tap in `ink-3`, aligned by the Optical Centre Rule. It opens About this song.
- **Row 2:** the artist (Subtitle), then, in Chords only, the song's own controls on the right. On wide screens that is the song's key pill (every song on screen has one). On phones it is the 1 4 5 / A B C segment and the 40px gear on `fill-hover`, since the key is in the bottom row; on wide screens those two settings, which apply to every song, sit in the top row instead.
- **Section chips:** a horizontal scroller of 32px capsules (hairline border, Caption 600, `ink-2`) that jump to a verse or chorus.

### Key Pill and Key Panel
- **Key pill:** a 40px capsule (56px glass in the bottom row) holding the key in Mono 1rem/700 and an offset badge. The badge is a 24px Mono capsule in `fill-on` and `chord` showing the semitone offset; at zero it is transparent and `ink-3`. With a capo it adds "Capo 2" in `ink-2`.
- **Key panel:** "Key · song title", 52px −/+ steppers around the key in Mono 2.25rem/700, a "+2 from original D" note, and a 4-column grid of 44px cells. Cells have a 12px radius and `fill-hover`. The selected cell is solid `chord` with `ground` text. The original key carries a 4px pixel 5px from the bottom. With Guitar capo on, a capo row (16px radius, `fill-hover`, stepper 0–9) shows which shapes to play. It ends with Reset and Done.

### Up Next
The bottom row's centre group: previous ‹, a label, and next ›. The label shows "Up next" (0.75rem, `ink-3`) over the next song's name (Label, truncated) and opens the setlist. When the last section of the song comes on screen, the group lights up: its tint mixes in 24% of `chord`. On the last song it reads "End of the set".

### Sidebar (wide)
- **Head:** "Setlist" (Title), "Thu 29 Oct · 6 songs" (Caption, `ink-2`), and an Edit order text button.
- **Rows:** song rows at 0.9375rem. In split songs both songs on screen are lit (`fill-on`, 600 title, `pixel-on`), and a pinned right song is tagged Pinned. In one song the next song is tagged Next. Tags are 0.6875rem/600 `chord` on a `fill-hover` capsule.
- **Footer:** under a hairline: previous and next taps around ← → keycaps and "Change songs".
- **Edit order:** adds grips, and a footer saying the order changes only this device, with Reset.

### Set Row (wide, sidebar folded)
The sidebar laid out horizontally. It is a 48px glass row with the sidebar's 24px corner, 10px under the top row and spanning the frame, so the songs below move down by 58px.
- **Songs:** each is a 40px capsule (4px in, so concentric) holding an 8px pixel in its three states, the number in Mono `ink-3`, the title (truncated) and the key in Mono `ink-2` at the right.
- **Width:** songs share the row evenly, at least 170px each, and scroll sideways for long sets with the current song kept in view. At 1440px six songs fit.
- **On screen:** the songs on screen are lit (`fill-on`, 600, `pixel-on`). Every song carries its full title as a tooltip.
- **Edit order:** a text button at the row's end opens the setlist popover in reorder mode.
- **Why a row:** a bar inside the top row was tried first. With the controls beside it, it had room for about 80px per song, so it could only title the songs already on screen, which repeated the panes.

### Song Rows (sidebar and setlist sheet)
A `12px | 22px | 1fr | auto` grid: the song pixel (10px, in the three pixel states), the number (`ink-3`, Mono), the title (truncated), and the key (`ink-2`, Mono 600). Rows are at least 48px tall with 6 × 10px padding. Hover is `fill-hover`. The current row (`aria-current`) gets a `fill-on` wash, a 600-weight title, and a `pixel-on` pixel.
- **Reorder:** each row gains a 28 × 36px grip of six 3px pixels. Drag it, or focus it and use ↑ ↓. The new order is saved on this device only.

### Sheets and Popovers
One panel, two forms. Each is strong glass with a 32px radius, 24px blur and 18 / 16px padding, and is headed by a Title, an optional Caption line, and a close tap.
- **Phones (sheets):** inset 10px from the sides, 10px above the bottom row, at most 560px wide and `min(70svh, 620px)` tall, over a 0.32 scrim.
  - **Open:** from `translateY(18px) scale(0.96)` at the bottom centre, with opacity over 160ms and transform over 480ms on the liquid curve.
  - **Close:** over 420ms.
  - **Rows:** the Reading sheet lists its rows bottom-up, so the most-used row is nearest the thumb.
- **Wide (popovers):** 360px, anchored to the control that opened them, growing from it (`--origin`), with no scrim.
- **Panels:**
  - **Reading (Aa):** text size, layout (split or one song) and lyric columns (one or two) on wide screens, auto-scroll, keep awake, theme, language, and Present to TV.
  - **Key:** described above.
  - **Chord settings (gear):** numbers or letters, full or simple chords, colour chords, sharps or flats, and guitar capo.
  - **Setlist:** the song rows with Edit order.
  - **About this song:** three fact tiles (Original key, Time, Tempo; 16px radius, `fill-hover`, Mono values) and credits.
  - **Choose a song:** the split picker.
- **Settings rows:** 56px, 16px radius, `fill-hover`, a bold label with an optional `ink-3` hint, and a control on the right (a segment, a stepper with 40px taps, or a 46 × 28px switch whose knob springs over 360ms).
- **Focus:** opening moves focus into the panel; Escape closes it and returns focus to its trigger.

### Done
- **Done:** the only filled button, a 48px `ink` capsule with `ground` text. It presses to `scale(0.98)`. A `fill-hover` ghost capsule (Reset) sits beside it.

### Split Panes (wide)
Both panes carry equal weight: neither is dimmed, and neither is "now" or "next". Each opens with a chooser naming its place in the set ("Song 3 of 6 ⌄"), a 32px Caption-600 button with a 10px radius, that shows any song there. A song chosen for the right is pinned ("Song 6 of 6 · Pinned", with a 6px `chord` pin pixel): it stays while the left pane moves through the set with ← →. Unpinned, → slides the right song into the left pane over 520ms and brings in the next. The key and About panels act on the pane they were opened from.

### Auto-scroll, Keep Awake and Toast
- **Auto-scroll:** slow, medium and fast scroll at 16, 28 and 44px a second. While it runs, a 44px `glass-tint-over` pill floats 10px above the bottom row (16px from the bottom on wide screens) with pause and stop. Touching, wheeling or pressing a key (other than ← →) stops it.
- **Keep awake:** on by default through the Screen Wake Lock API, released when the page is hidden and taken again when it returns. Where it is not supported, the row says so.
- **Toast:** a 44px strong-glass capsule 14px below the top row, with a pixel check in `chord` and UI Strong text. It enters from `translate(−50%, −10px) scale(0.9)` over 480ms on the liquid curve.

### Present to TV
A full-screen view for a shared screen: lyrics only by default, one section at a time, on `ground` with the top wash.
- **Top row:** the song title in `ink-3`, with the controls on the right.
- **Middle:** the section label over the lines, centred and sized down until they fit. The first slide of each song is a title slide: "Song 2 of 6" in Mono, the title, then the artist.
- **Foot:** what comes next ("Next: …" for the next line, "Next song: …" at a song's end) and a row of 22 × 4px progress pixels.
- **Controls:** Show chords and Exit are 40px glass capsules. They fade in on pointer movement and hide after 2.2s.
- **Moving:** → ↓ Page Down Space Enter go forward, ← ↑ Page Up Backspace go back, and the same keys work on Bluetooth page-turner pedals. Clicking the right two-thirds goes forward. C toggles chords, and Esc or leaving full screen exits.
- **Animation:** each slide enters from 14px below over 420ms.

### Chords
Chords show as Nashville numbers relative to the song's original key by default ("1 4 5"), or as letters in the chosen key. Simple drops 7ths, sus and slash basses. Sharps & flats picks the spelling. With a capo, letters show the shapes to play. Superscripts sit at 0.68em.

### Motion
- **Easing:** two curves. Ease-out, `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, is for colour, opacity and fades. Liquid, `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.32, 0.52, 1)`, is for anything glass that moves or scales, and overshoots slightly.
- **Durations:** hover fills take 160–180ms, presses 260ms, the segmented thumb 480ms, panels 420–480ms, and pixel state 400ms.
- **Reduced motion:** under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, every transition and animation runs in 1ms, and scrolling jumps instead of gliding.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep neutrals hue-free (`oklch(L 0 0)`) and derive every coloured token from `--accent`.
- **Do** float controls as glass groups: put related controls in one capsule, keep unrelated ones apart, and leave the rows that hold them clear.
- **Do** keep nested corners concentric: inner radius = outer radius − inset.
- **Do** keep everything a phone player needs mid-song in the bottom row, and open panels from the control that triggered them.
- **Do** show a set pixel's state by brightness: `pixel-off` to come, `pixel-played` played, `pixel-on` now.
- **Do** build glass as a hybrid: the CSS backdrop-filter stack everywhere, with bezel refraction from size-cached SVG maps in Chromium. Honour reduced transparency, increased contrast and forced colours.
- **Do** draw pixelarticons (MIT) at `var(--icon)`, with `fill="currentColor"` and `shape-rendering: crispEdges`.
- **Do** set every word in Geist, controls included; keep Geist Mono for chords, keys, offsets, counts, row numbers, steppers, keycaps and the notation segment.
- **Do** spell accidentals `b` and `#`.
- **Do** self-host Geist (400/500/600/700) and Geist Mono (500/600/700) in production, keeping the PingFang TC / Noto Sans TC fallbacks for 繁體中文.
- **Do** keep tap targets at least 40px, and 44px or more for rows, key cells and standalone glass controls.
- **Do** dissolve content into a scroll-edge band before it reaches floating controls.

### Don't:
- **Don't** give songs, sections or states their own colour, tint the greys, or let colour chords tint anything but chord text.
- **Don't** put a full-width glass bar behind controls, or join unrelated controls in one piece of glass.
- **Don't** put lyrics, titles or reading text inside glass, or in a bordered sheet or card.
- **Don't** nest glass inside glass. Controls inside glass are fills.
- **Don't** show the next song's lyrics below the current song on a phone; Up next names it instead.
- **Don't** dim one of the split songs or call one "now" and the other "next"; both are read at once.
- **Don't** hide the setlist on wide screens; fold it into the set row. Don't squeeze it into the top row either: there the titles don't fit, and showing only the songs already on screen repeats the panes.
- **Don't** round a pixel, or replace a square pixel with a dot.
- **Don't** draw icons at a fixed size, or use any icon set other than pixelarticons.
- **Don't** set control labels, prose, headings, section names or song titles in Geist Mono, or use a Light weight for lyrics.
- **Don't** use the section-label style (uppercase, 0.12em tracking) for anything but song section names.
- **Don't** use ♭ or ♯ glyphs.
- **Don't** implement glass with WebGL or SVG-only filters, or recompute refraction maps every frame.
- **Don't** reuse the old navy palette from `ui/src/styles.css`, or the retired ink-blue tokens.
