Split view: top-bar button and per-song columns

Desktop shown at 55%, tablet landscape at 60%, TV (1920 × 1080) at 43%. Moving pair (A) and header D in the panes, as proposed in round 1.

1 · Split view button in the top bar

A two-pane icon button sits between Lyrics | Chords and Aa on desktop and tablet landscape, and it is no longer in Aa. Off: the same soft button as share. On: accent wash with a thin accent ring, so the pressed state reads at a glance without a second navy moment. Hover shows its name and the S shortcut. Phone and tablet portrait don't show it.

2 · Columns per song in split view: where it fits

Columns stay a per-song setting that follows the song from pane to pane (Be Thou My Vision keeps two columns when it moves from Next to Now). The limit is width: each pane is half the screen, and a lyric column needs about 30 characters before hymn lines start to wrap.

3 · Ways to offer it

S1, a per-song column switch in each pane header, shown only when that pane can actually hold two columns (TV and large monitors, or a smaller text size). Elsewhere it hides, like the Letters-only settings. S2, a weighted split: a song set to two columns gets 60% of the width. S3, Auto only: each pane picks one or two columns for its own song with no control at all.

Recommendation: yes, keep columns per song, but S1 and S2 together, and only for lyrics on big screens. The column choice belongs to the song and follows it from pane to pane. A pane shows the small One | Two switch only when its song could fit two columns at a 60% share (a TV, a large monitor, or a smaller text size). Choosing Two gives that pane 60% of the width; if both songs choose Two, the panes go back to 50 / 50 and Auto falls back to one column wherever lines would wrap. On a 1440 laptop and on tablet landscape, the switch never appears and each pane is one column. Chords stay one column per pane in split view.
