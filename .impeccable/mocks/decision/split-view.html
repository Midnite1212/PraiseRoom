RIPPLE OUT · PARTICIPANT AND MUSICIAN VIEW · SPLIT VIEW

Split view: two songs side by side

At most two songs. Desktop shown at 55%, tablet landscape at 60%. Same Calm × Paper rules: two sheets on the tinted layer, no lines around lyrics, one navy moment (Lyrics | Chords).

1 · Turning it on

Split view is a switch in the Aa menu, offered only where two songs fit side by side: desktop and tablet landscape (phone and tablet portrait never show it). While it is on, each song is one column, so the Layout row hides. Nothing new appears in the top bar.

2 · Participant view

Two reading sheets side by side. The list moves above as the progress strip (both songs on screen are marked, joined into one bar) so each song gets about 600px of reading width on desktop. The setlist can still sit beside, at the cost of narrower panes. A small NOW / NEXT label tops each pane.

3 · How the two panes move

A: the pair moves together. The left pane is always the current song and the right pane the next one; ← → or a click in the list moves both by one song. No focus to manage. B: two independent panes. Click a pane to focus it (a thin accent line on top); the list and ← → change only that pane. Lets you hold any two songs, e.g. 02 and a reprise of 05.

Recommendation: A, the moving pair. It needs no focus state and always answers the question both participants and musicians have mid-song: what is now and what comes next. B suits medleys and reprises out of order, but adds a mode to understand on a shared TV.

4 · Musician view: chord controls split in two

Key is per song, so each pane keeps its own key chip. Numbers | Letters and chord settings apply to both songs, so in split view they move up to the top bar next to Lyrics | Chords. This also settles the header question: with only the key chip left, N and D both stay calm in a narrow pane.

5 · Long title in a pane (tablet landscape, the narrowest case)

"Great Is Thy Faithfulness (Morning by Morning)" in the right pane, lyrics shown as placeholders.
