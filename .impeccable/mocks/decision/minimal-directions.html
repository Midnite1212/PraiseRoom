Three minimal directions, from scratch

Kept from earlier decisions: Overpass with Source Code Pro, E4 colours, one song at a time with an Up next control on phones and one song list on larger screens, lyrics-only by default. Everything else is new. Lyrics shown at Overpass 300.

1 · Paper

Minimal chrome. Almost nothing is on screen but the song: the setlist name, the title and the lyrics. Lyrics | Chords, text size and theme fold into one "Aa" menu (shown open in the third phone); the song count sits top-left; "Next · It Is Well" is a quiet text link at the bottom. Reference feel: Apple Books, Notes.

2 · Grid

Minimal structure. Everything stays visible, but on a strict left-aligned grid with hairline rules and small 6px outlined controls; numbers in mono. Nothing is filled except the one "on" state. Reference feel: a well-set printed programme.

3 · Calm

Minimal tone. No lines at all: the page sits on a soft powder tint, the header and controls are gentle fills, the current song is a single navy fill, and the Up next bar floats on a soft shadow. Reference feel: a quiet native app.
