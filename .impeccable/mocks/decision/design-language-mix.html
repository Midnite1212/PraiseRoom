Crisp & Precise + Open Editorial: two mixes

One rule for both: the page reads like print, and anything tappable is crisp. They differ in how the header and song navigation are framed. Deep Navy & Clear Sky colours; Sora, Plus Jakarta Sans and Geist Mono.

A Editorial page, crisp controls

The page reads like print: no cards, hairline rules, section labels that run into a rule. Everything tappable is crisp: 6px corners, 1px outlines, a solid fill for "on". Songs are outlined chips; the current one gets the sky outline and wash.

B Crisp frame, editorial content

A crisp header panel (title, actions, controls) sits above an open page. Songs are editorial underline tabs, with the current song underlined in sky blue. The lyrics are open and rule-separated. Quieter, with more of a "document" feel.
