Desktop song header: options

Four ways to shorten the song title and controls on desktop, each shown in the participant and musician views. Chord lines use the new, slightly tighter spacing.

H0 · Current, for comparison

Label, 36–40px title, meta line, then (musician) a full-width chord bar: four rows before the first lyric.

H1 · Compact

One small label line (song position, writer, time, tempo) above a 30px title. In the musician view the chord controls sit on the title row, so the separate chord bar is gone. Saves about two rows.

H2 · One line

Position, a 26px title, ⓘ and a short meta on a single line, with the chord controls at the right of the same line. The shortest header that still lives in the sheet.

H3 · Title in the top bar

The song title moves into the centre of the top bar and the chord controls join its right side; the reading sheet starts straight with lyrics. The most space back, but the top bar gets busier, especially in the musician view.
