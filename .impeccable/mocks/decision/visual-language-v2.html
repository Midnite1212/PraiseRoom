Three border-free visual languages

Palette E4 (Dusty Navy & Powder Sky), light and dark. Overpass with lyrics at 200 and headings at 300 (controls at 400–500), plus Geist Mono. No 1px outlines anywhere: separation comes from fills, tone, shadow and space. The lyrics stay an open page in all three.

1 · Tonal

No outlines anywhere. Controls sit on soft tinted fills; toggles have a sliding white thumb; floating things (the Up next bar, the sheet, the card) get a gentle shadow instead of a border. Section labels lose their rule lines; spacing does the separating.

2 · Navy-anchored

The header becomes a solid dusty-navy block with white type and translucent controls; the Up next bar and the card share the navy. Bolder and more branded, with the lyrics page staying light and open.

3 · Pill & air

Pill shapes for every control, frameless icon buttons, song tabs as pill chips, and generous space. The softest and lightest of the three; almost nothing is drawn, just shapes of tone.
