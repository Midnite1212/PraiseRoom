RIPPLE OUT WORSHIP · DESIGN SPEC

Public setlist view
Calm × Paper

Every page and layout of the participant view (lyrics only), as decided on 14 October 2026. Phone, tablet and desktop, in light and dark. The musician (chords) view comes next and is not included.

Type

Overpass for all text (lyrics at 300). Source Code Pro for labels, keys and numbers.

Colour

E4 Dusty Navy & Powder Sky. Flat, light and dark.

Two layers

Controls on a soft tinted chrome layer; lyrics on a reading sheet with 24px rounded top corners. No lines around lyrics.

Controls

Gentle fills, 12px corners. One navy moment: the active Lyrics | Chords option. Current song = soft accent wash.

Phone

Minimal top bar, thin progress segments, floating Up next card. Tap it for the song list. Swipe to change songs.

Desktop

Contained setlist rail; a layout icon moves it into a labelled progress strip. ⓘ opens song details. Aa holds text size, columns and theme. ← → change songs.

Lyrics shown are public-domain hymns used as sample content. “Thursday Care Group” is a sample setlist.
