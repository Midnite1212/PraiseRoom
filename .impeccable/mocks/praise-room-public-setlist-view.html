How it's laid out

  • Floating glass. No full-width bars: each group of controls floats as its own piece of glass, so more of the song shows between them.
  • Top: set once. Lyrics | Chords and share. Thin segments show where you are in the set; tap one to jump.
  • Bottom: in thumb reach. Aa, Up next with previous and next, and the key in the musician view. Each opens its panel upward from where you tapped.
  • Header D. Row 1 is the title and ⓘ; row 2 is the artist with 1 4 5 | A B C and chord settings. Section chips jump to a verse or chorus.
  • Up next lights up. Near the end of a song, Up next names the next one. Tap › to move on, or swipe sideways.

Try this

  1. Switch to Chords, then tap E +2 at the bottom right to change key.
  2. Open ⚙ and turn on Colour chords, Simple or Guitar capo.
  3. Tap Aa for text size, auto-scroll, theme and 繁體中文.
  4. Scroll to the end of the song and watch Up next name the next one.
  5. Tap Up next for the song list, then Edit order.

Split songs

Two songs side by side, both full strength, each with its own key. → slides the right song over and brings in the next. "Song 3 of 6 ⌄" over either pane picks any song; one picked for the right stays pinned while the left moves on.

Sidebar or top bar

The setlist sits in the sidebar, with both songs on screen lit. The list button at the top left folds it into a set row under the top bar, every song with its title and key, so the songs get the full width. Edit order reorders the set for you alone. ← → and page-turner pedals work too.

Aa and Present

Aa holds text size, layout (split or one song), lyric columns (one or two, remembered for each layout), auto-scroll, keep-awake, theme and language. Present to TV shows one section at a time; C toggles chords, Esc leaves.

This is a design mock, not the production app. Settings are saved in this browser only.
