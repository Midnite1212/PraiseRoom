Each screen below shows the baseline on the left and my proposal beside it. Every change is numbered and explained at the end, so you can take or leave each one on its own.

Brainstorm: frequent controls at the bottom

Frequent mid-song: previous / next, text size, auto-scroll, and key (musicians). Once a visit: Lyrics | Chords, notation and chord style. Rarely: theme, share, sign in. Each option below moves the frequent ones into thumb reach.

B1 in detail

Your answers: Lyrics | Chords is set once (it stays at the top), auto-scroll is an extra (it lives behind Aa), and the bottom bar mainly serves the phone in hand. Tablets in portrait get the same bar, just wider.

Things any bottom design must respect: keep 44px tap targets; leave room above the phone's home indicator and Safari's own bottom toolbar; don't fight the swipe-up-to-home gesture; and keep previous / next in the same place in every option so musicians can find them without looking.

The changes

1Tappable progress segments

Tap or drag a segment to jump to that song, with the song's name shown while you hold. Today they only show position.

Cost: small. Segments need a bigger touch area than they look.

2Section chips

Verse / Chorus / Bridge chips under the artist jump straight there, for when the leader calls "back to the chorus".

Cost: small. Hidden when a song has one section.

3Up next does more

Swipe the reading sheet left or right to change song; tapping the middle still opens the song list. The card shows the next song's key in the musician view.

Cost: small. Swipe stays a hidden extra.

4Flow on at the end

After the last line, the next song's title, key and opening lines follow, dimmed. Scrolling into it makes it current, so you see what's coming without tapping.

Cost: medium. Needs a smooth hand-off so the page doesn't jump.

5Key pill shows the change

"E +2" (and "Capo 2" when set) instead of "Key D ▾", so a musician sees the transposition without opening the panel. With B1 it sits in the bottom bar on phones and in the header on wide screens.

Cost: none. Same control, more information.

6Auto-scroll in reach

Started from Aa (it's an extra). While it runs, a slim pause · speed · stop pill appears above the bar; touching the lyrics pauses it.

Cost: small.

7Split the settings by purpose

The gear keeps chord options only (notation, full or simple, colour, sharps and flats, capo). Auto-scroll and keep-awake move to Aa, which is about reading, so participants find them too.

Cost: none. Mostly a move.

8Keep awake on by default, plus Present

Screens shouldn't dim mid-song, so keep-awake starts on (it can still be switched off). Present to TV joins the Aa menu.

Cost: small.

9Reorder for yourself

Edit order in the song list, with drag handles. It's saved on this device only and labelled "Your order", with Reset.

Cost: medium.

10The strip marks now and next

When songs are split, the strip above labels the current and next songs, and you can tap any song to put it on the right.

Cost: small. It builds on the baseline's strip.

11Split songs (left | right)

The current song on the left and the next on the right, quieter. Start (or →) slides the right one over. Its title picks another song to pin there.

Cost: medium. Half width wraps long lines at large text sizes.

12Split is a layout choice

"Split songs" joins Auto / One / Two in the Aa Layout setting, and landscape tablets start in it.

Cost: none. It reuses the existing control.

13Header: D everywhere

Row 1: title and ⓘ. Row 2: artist left, chord controls right. One shape for every song and screen, so controls never move between songs. Long titles wrap within row 1.

Cost: small.

14Present to TV

Full screen, lyrics only, one section at a time, with the next line previewed. Show chords is a toggle.

Cost: medium.

15Offline state

The opened setlist is saved on the device; when the connection drops, a quiet notice appears and the songs keep working.

Cost: medium (needs offline caching).

16Bottom bar on phones (B1)

Aa · Up next · key in one bar at the bottom; Aa and Key open their panels upward. Lyrics | Chords and share stay at the top because they're set once. Tablets in portrait use the same bar.

Cost: small. It reshapes the existing Up next card.
