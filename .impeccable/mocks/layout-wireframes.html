Public setlist view: layout wireframes

Pick one layout per screen. The structure is already decided (one song with flow-on on phones, two songs side by side on wide screens, and Present for the TV); these options only change where things sit. Dashed boxes are areas, solid boxes are controls, and the dark box is the one filled button.

Phone (portrait)

Every option keeps: previous and next in thumb reach, the next song's opening flowing on after the end, and the named next button.

Wide screens (split)

Every option keeps: the current song on the left, the next or pinned song on the right, and the slide when you advance.

TV (Present)

Every option keeps: lyrics only by default, the Show chords toggle, and keyboard, clicker or pedal to advance.
